The Problem

University cafeterias waste 30–40% of prepared meals daily — an environmental, financial and ethical crisis. Static menu planning and lack of real-time student feedback drive over-production. Our system uses AI & machine learning to predict demand, capture anonymous preferences and optimize menus to reduce waste at the source.

Project Goals
  • Predict daily food waste with ensemble ML
  • Optimize weekly menu via multi-objective AI
  • Capture anonymous student feedback at scale
  • Explain predictions through SHAP-based XAI
  • Reduce cost, carbon footprint & food waste

Conclusion

We delivered a production-ready AI platform that unifies waste prediction, menu optimization and anonymous student feedback into one ecosystem. Trained on 816 real production records and 2,573 ratings, our ensemble model achieves an MAE of 2.6 portions, with student ratings ranked as the #1 most important feature (25% impact). The system demonstrates that data-driven cafeterias can meaningfully cut food waste while improving meal satisfaction.

Future Improvements
  • Computer vision for automatic plate-waste measurement
  • LSTM / Transformer models for time-series demand
  • Personalized nutrition recommendations
  • Carbon footprint & sustainability scoring
  • Multi-campus deployment with federated learning
